Nutrients

How to read this

Mixed is the default: each nutrient is averaged over the time your body holds on to it. Energy counts per day, protein, fiber and potassium over 3 days, zinc and vitamin K over 7, vitamin C, magnesium and omega 3 over 14, and stored nutrients like B12, vitamin D, iron, calcium and iodine over 30 days. Day, 7 days and 30 days show one period for everything.

Bar colors show which meal a nutrient came from. The line marks 100 %. Tap a nutrient for details.

Bars show how much of your daily need your day reaches. now added lost below need lost but still covered. The line marks 100 %.
